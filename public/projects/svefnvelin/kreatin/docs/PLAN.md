# Creatine atlas implementation plan

Goal: Icelandic animated educational site using the supplied BodyParts3D anatomy, with all 18 dose/duration combinations and a detailed, evidence-calibrated brain view.

Design: a dark scientific instrument; large anatomical stage, compact left controls, contextual right explanations, an interactive time rail and evidence library. Mint muscles and cyan brain; restrained amber uncertainty. Three views: body, muscle cell, brain. Native buttons and range inputs support keyboard use. Reduced-motion preference pauses illustrative loops.

Architecture: static ES modules, locally vendored Three.js, source-derived merged anatomical buffers. A pure educational state module supplies qualitative phases, never personalized predictions. Visual geometry changes are deliberately illustrative and labeled. Separate animated mechanisms explain energy transfer, hydration, adaptation and uncertain recovery. No dose-based cognitive improvement meter.

Implementation sequence:
1. Extract muscular/skeletal/brain geometry from supplied atlas, preserving provenance and source identities; do not modify source assets.
2. Test the 18 scenarios, saturation plateau, high-dose cautions and lack of numerical individual predictions.
3. Author body view and controls; show meaningful local preview.
4. Add cell and brain mechanisms, guided timeline, comparison, accessible pause/reset and evidence drawer.
5. Verify desktop/mobile, every scenario, keyboard, reduced motion, pause, view transitions, local asset loading and console errors.
6. Publish private Site and retain reproducible source, evidence notes and checks.

Evidence boundaries: 20 g/day remains the selected dose across the entire chosen period (not silently switched to maintenance). Typical loading is separately explained. 10 g timeline is uncertain interpolation for display only. Healthy adult resistance training three times weekly assumed. Balanced diet, enough protein/energy and progressive training assumed, not quantified. No individual weight/strength/cognition predictions. Three months to a year uses qualitative maintenance rather than extrapolated growth.
